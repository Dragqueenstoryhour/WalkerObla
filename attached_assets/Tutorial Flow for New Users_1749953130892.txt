Tutorial Flow for New Users
This tutorial will guide you through the key features of Speech Practice.
1. Welcome & Topic Selection:

   * "Welcome to Speech Practice! Start by selecting a topic from the list to generate words, or use the input field to create your own custom topic."
   * (Points to: Topic selection dropdown/area, then Custom topic input field)
   2. Practice Cards & Recording:

      * "Here are your practice cards, each displaying a word to speak. When you're ready, click the 'Start Recording' button to begin practicing."
      * (Points to: The entire practice card area, then highlights the 'Start Recording' button. Tutorial pauses, user must click 'Start Recording' to proceed.)
      * "Now speak the word clearly. Click the 'Stop Recording' button when you're finished, and you'll see your pronunciation results."
      * (Points to: 'Stop Recording' button)
      3. Assistance Tools:

         * "To help you, you can click 'Hear' to listen to the word, toggle 'Slow' to slow down the pronunciation, or click 'See' for an articulation animation."
         * (Points to: 'Hear' button, then the 'Slow' switch/icon, then the 'See' button)
         4. Navigation Between Words:

            * "Once you've practiced a word, click the 'Next' button to move to the next word. You can also go back with the 'Previous' button."
            * (Points to: 'Next' button, then briefly highlights 'Previous' button)
            5. Saving Words to My Journey:

               * "If you find a word you want to practice again later, simply click the 'Save' button to add it to your 'My Journey' list."
               * (Points to: 'Save' button)
               6. Adjusting Difficulty Level:

                  * "To change the difficulty of the words presented, click the 'Words Level' button and use the slider to adjust your preference."
                  * (Points to: 'Words Level' button, then the difficulty slider/bar that appears)
